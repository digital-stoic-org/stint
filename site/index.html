Procedure 1

How a stint runs

  1. 1

    Watch the revs.

    A band above your prompt shows how full the context window is. At 80k tokens it turns 🔥: plan your pit. At 120k, 🚨: box this lap.

    eyes on the band
  2. 2

    Pit.

    /pit: the agent writes its notes, the context clears itself, and the notes come back as the first message of a fresh window. Driver change, same race.

    one command, no tools needed
  3. 3

    Rejoin.

    /rejoin: next morning, new session, same stream. The notes go in first.

    pick up where you left off

Glossary

Pit-lane terms

Glossary · pit-lane terms
TermOn trackIn stint
revsEngine revolutions per minute: how hard the engine works.How full the context window is, shown in the band.
redlineThe top of the rev counter, where engines break.Where the agent starts to lose the thread. The band turns 🚨 before it.
lapOne turn of the circuit.One tool call, its result, and the agent's reasoning.
stintThe run between two pit stops.One context window, from fresh to pit.
box / pitThe radio call to come in, then the stop./pit writes the notes and clears the window.
driver changeA fresh driver, same car, same race.A fresh window that starts from the notes.
